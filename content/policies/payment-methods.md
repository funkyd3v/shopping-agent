# Payment Methods Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-11-01

## Accepted payment methods

| Method | Available | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Visa | Yes | Credit and debit |
| Mastercard | Yes | Credit and debit |
| American Express | Yes | Credit only |
| PayPal | Yes | US and Canada accounts |
| Cash on delivery | No | Not offered |
| Gift cards | No | Not offered at launch |
| Bank transfer | No | Not offered at launch |

All prices and charges are in **US dollars (USD)**. Canadian orders are charged in USD and may incur a currency-conversion fee from the card issuer.

## When you are charged

We authorize the payment method when the order is placed and capture the charge when the order ships. A temporary authorization hold may appear immediately and usually disappears within **3–5 business days** if the order is cancelled before shipment.

If an item is out of stock after the order is placed, we cancel that line and capture only the amount for the items that ship.

## Declined payments

If a payment is declined, the order is not placed and no stock is reserved. Common reasons are an expired card, a billing-address mismatch, or a bank fraud block. Contact your bank first, then try again with the same or a different method.

## Refund destination

Refunds always go back to the original payment method. We cannot send a refund to a different card or account. Refund timing is described in the Returns and Refunds Policy and typically takes **5–7 business days** after inspection.

## Security

We never ask for your full card number by email or chat. Payment details are handled by our payment processor; support can see only the last four digits and the card brand. Do not send card numbers, CVVs, or passwords in chat.

## Taxes and fees

Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on the shipping address. Shipping charges are shown before you place the order. There are no hidden service fees. Duties for Canadian orders are charged separately by the carrier.
