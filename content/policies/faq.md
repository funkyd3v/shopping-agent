## Orders

**How much can I order at once?**
A single order can total up to **$200.00** and can contain up to **5 units** of any one item. Larger orders should be split into separate orders.

**Can I change or cancel an order?**
You can cancel from the order page within **30 minutes** of placing it. After that window, the order is being processed and cannot be cancelled through the store. See the Cancellation Policy.

**Do you take phone orders?**
No. Orders are placed on the website or through the shopping assistant chat.

**Why was my order refused?**
The most common reasons are an order total above **$200.00**, more than **5 units** of one item, an out-of-stock line, or a missing shipping address. The store explains the specific reason and does not create a partial order.

## Shipping

**When will my order arrive?**
Standard shipping takes **3–5 business days** after a **1 business day** processing period. Expedited shipping takes **1–2 business days**.

**Is shipping free?**
Standard shipping is free on US orders of **$50.00 or more**. Below that, standard shipping is **$5.95**.

**Do you ship internationally?**
We ship to the **United States and Canada** only. Canadian standard shipping takes **5–7 business days** and costs **$12.95**, free over **$75.00**.

**How do I track my order?**
A tracking link is emailed within **24 hours** of shipment. Tracking can take **48 hours** to update.

## Returns and refunds

**What is the return window?**
**30 calendar days** from delivery for most items. Opened personal-care items and final-sale items cannot be returned.

**How long does a refund take?**
**5–7 business days** after the returned item is inspected. The bank may take another **3–5 business days** to post it.

**Who pays return shipping?**
We do for damaged, defective, or wrong items. Otherwise return shipping is free for orders of **$50.00 or more**; smaller orders have **$4.95** deducted from the refund.

## Payments

**Which cards do you accept?**
Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal. We do not accept cash, gift cards, or bank transfers.

**When am I charged?**
The card is authorized at checkout and charged when the order ships.

**Can I use a different card for a refund?**
No. Refunds go to the original payment method.

## Products and support

**What warranty comes with a product?**
A **12-month limited warranty** by default. Some products list **24 months** on the product page.

**How do I contact support?**
Email **support@shopping-agent.example**, Monday–Friday, **9:00 AM–5:00 PM Eastern Time**. We reply within **1 business day**.

**Is my payment information safe?**
Yes. We never store full card numbers, and support never asks for card details in chat.
