# Order Cancellation Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-11-01

## Cancellation window

You can cancel an order within **30 minutes** of placing it, as long as its status is still **placed**. The order page shows the exact deadline in your local time. Once the window closes or the status changes to **shipped**, the order cannot be cancelled through the store.

## How to cancel

| Where | How | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Order page | Choose **Cancel order** before the deadline | Status becomes cancelled and stock is restored |
| Shopping assistant chat | Say “cancel order ORD-XXXXX” | Same result, recorded as an assistant action |
| Email support | Only after the window, as a request | Not guaranteed; may become a return instead |

Cancelling is immediate and does not require a confirmation step. Cancelling an order that is already cancelled returns the existing cancelled state.

## Refunds after cancellation

| Payment state | What happens |
|---|---|
| Authorized but not captured | The authorization hold is released; it may take 3–5 business days to disappear |
| Captured | Refunded to the original payment method within 5–7 business days |

Shipping charges are refunded in full when an order is cancelled before shipment.

## After the window

After 30 minutes, the order may already be in the fulfilment queue. Contact support and we will try to intercept it, but cancellation is not guaranteed. If the order ships, use the Returns and Refunds Policy: most items can be returned within **30 days** of delivery.

## Cancellation and stock

Cancelling restores every item to inventory in the same transaction as the status change. If an item sold out in the meantime, the restored stock is available to other shoppers.

## Orders placed by the assistant

Orders placed through the shopping assistant follow the same **30-minute** window and appear on the orders page with an **AI assistant** badge. You can cancel them the same way as orders placed on the website.

## Repeated cancellations

There is no fee for cancelling. However, repeatedly placing and cancelling orders may trigger a manual review of the account. The assistant can place at most **5 orders per day** per user.
