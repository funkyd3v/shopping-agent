# Returns and Refunds Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-11-01

## Return window

Most items can be returned within **30 calendar days** of delivery. The window starts on the delivery date shown by the carrier, not the order date. Items returned after 30 days are not accepted unless they are covered by a warranty claim.

## Condition requirements

To receive a full refund, an item must be:

- unused and in the same condition it arrived,
- returned with all original packaging, manuals, and accessories,
- accompanied by the order number or packing slip.

Items that show wear, missing parts, or damage caused after delivery may receive a partial refund or be refused.

## Non-returnable items

| Category | Returnable? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Opened personal-care items | No | Hygiene reasons |
| Digital downloads and gift cards | No | Delivered electronically |
| Final-sale items | No | Marked on the product page |
| Assembled furniture | Yes, with fee | 15% restocking fee |
| Consumables (coffee, cleaning supplies) | No if opened | Unopened packs are returnable |

## How to start a return

1. Open the order in your account and choose **Return items**.
2. Select the items and a reason.
3. Print the prepaid label (emailed within **1 business day**).
4. Drop the package at the carrier within **14 days** of receiving the label.

Return shipping is free for orders of **$50.00 or more**. For smaller orders, **$4.95** is deducted from the refund. Expedited shipping charges are not refunded unless the return is our error.

## Refund timing

| Step | Time |
|---|---|
| Carrier transit back to warehouse | 3–7 business days |
| Inspection | 1–2 business days after arrival |
| Refund issued to the original payment method | 5–7 business days after inspection |

We refund the merchandise price plus tax. Original shipping is refunded only when the return is due to a damaged, defective, or incorrect item. You receive an email when the refund is issued; your bank may take an additional **3–5 business days** to post it.

## Exchanges

We do not process direct exchanges. Return the original item for a refund and place a new order. This keeps stock accurate and gets the replacement to you faster.

## Damaged or wrong items

Contact support within **3 days** of delivery for damage and within **30 days** for a wrong item. Include photos where possible. We cover return shipping and send a replacement or full refund.
