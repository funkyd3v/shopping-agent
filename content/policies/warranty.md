# Warranty Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-11-01

## Standard coverage

Every new product includes a **12-month limited warranty** from the delivery date. Select products list a **24-month** warranty on the product page; the product page overrides this default.

The warranty covers manufacturing defects that appear during normal use, including:

- electrical or mechanical failure not caused by accident,
- faulty materials or workmanship,
- batteries that fail to hold a charge under normal use.

## What is not covered

| Exclusion | Example |
|---|---|
| Accidental damage | Drops, spills, cracked screens |
| Cosmetic wear | Scratches, faded finishes, stretched fabric |
| Misuse or modification | Unauthorized repairs, cut cables |
| Normal consumable wear | Worn ear pads, depleted batteries after 12 months |
| Lost or stolen items | No police report or carrier claim |
| Water damage on non-waterproof products | Submersion of a non-rated device |

## Warranty claim process

1. Contact support with the order number and a description of the fault.
2. Support may ask for photos, a short video, or basic troubleshooting.
3. If the claim is approved, we email a prepaid return label within **2 business days**.
4. After inspection, we repair, replace with the same or equivalent model, or refund the purchase price.

Claims must be started before the warranty period ends. A repair or replacement does not extend the original warranty period.

## Turnaround times

| Stage | Typical time |
|---|---|
| Initial response | 1 business day |
| Troubleshooting and approval | 1–3 business days |
| Return transit | 3–7 business days |
| Inspection and resolution | 2–5 business days |

## Extended and commercial use

Extended warranties are not sold at launch. Products used for commercial purposes carry the same 12-month limited warranty but are limited to **one replacement per order**. Consumable parts are covered for **90 days**.
