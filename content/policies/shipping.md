# Shipping Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-11-01
**Applies to:** orders shipped within the United States and Canada.

## Processing time

Orders placed before **2:00 PM Eastern Time** on a business day are processed the same day. Orders placed after the cutoff, on a weekend, or on a US federal holiday are processed on the next business day. Processing takes **1 business day**; it is separate from transit time.

## Shipping methods and rates

| Method | Delivery estimate | Cost | Free over |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3–5 business days | $5.95 | $50.00 |
| Expedited | 1–2 business days | $14.95 | Not eligible |
| Canada standard | 5–7 business days | $12.95 | $75.00 |

- Standard shipping is free when the merchandise subtotal is **$50.00 or more** before tax.
- Expedited shipping is available only for US addresses and is never free.
- Alaska, Hawaii, and US territories add **$9.95** and ship in 5–7 business days.
- We do not ship to PO boxes for expedited orders.

## Tracking

A tracking number is emailed when the carrier scans the package, usually within **24 hours** of shipment. Tracking can take up to **48 hours** to show movement. If tracking has not updated for **5 business days**, contact support.

## Delivery problems

| Problem | What to do | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Package marked delivered but missing | Check around the property, then contact support | Within 7 days of the delivery scan |
| Damaged in transit | Keep the packaging and contact support with photos | Within 3 days of delivery |
| Wrong item shipped | Contact support; we send a prepaid return label | Within 30 days of delivery |
| Lost in transit | We open a carrier trace and reship or refund | After 5 business days without movement |

## Address changes

We can change the shipping address only before the order enters processing. Because processing starts quickly, address changes are not guaranteed. Once a package ships, the address cannot be changed.

## International orders

At launch we ship only to the **United States and Canada**. Customers are responsible for any duties or taxes charged by Canadian authorities. Delivery estimates exclude customs delays.
