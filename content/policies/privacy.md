# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-11-01

## What we collect

| Data | Why | Required? |
|---|---|---|
| Name and email | Account, receipts, support | Yes |
| Shipping address | Delivery and tax | Yes |
| Order and cart history | Fulfilment and support | Yes |
| Chat messages | Answering questions and improving the demo | Yes for chat features |
| Payment token and last four digits | Charging and refunds | Yes for purchases |
| Device and log data | Security and debugging | Automatic |

We do not store full card numbers, CVVs, or bank credentials. Payment details are handled by our payment processor.

## How we use data

We use your data to create your account, fulfil orders, process returns, answer support questions, prevent fraud, and improve the store. We do not sell personal data. We do not use your data for third-party advertising.

## Sharing

We share the minimum data needed with:

- shipping carriers, to deliver orders;
- the payment processor, to charge and refund;
- infrastructure providers that host the store and database.

These providers may process data outside your state or country. We require them to protect it and use it only for the service they provide.

## Retention

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Account and order records | 24 months after the last order, then deleted or anonymized |
| Support and chat transcripts | 12 months |
| Security and access logs | 90 days |
| Payment records | As required by tax and accounting law |

You can request deletion of your account at any time. We may retain order records where law requires it.

## Your choices

You can access or correct your profile and address in your account. You can request a copy or deletion of your data by emailing **privacy@shopping-agent.example**. We respond within **30 days**.

## Cookies

We use a single httpOnly session cookie to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers. Clearing cookies signs you out.

## Children

The store is not directed to children under **13**. We do not knowingly collect data from children. Contact us if you believe a child created an account.

## Demo notice

This is a demonstration store. Use fake personal data. Payments are mocked and no real charge is made.
